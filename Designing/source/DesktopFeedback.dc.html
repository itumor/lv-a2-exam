Corrections

Es biju uz koncerts.
Es biju koncertā.

To say where you were, use the locative ending: koncerts becomes koncertā. No preposition is needed.

Man patīk mūziku.
Man patika mūzika.

With patikt, the thing you like stays in the nominative. The concert is over, so use the past tense patika.

Missing plan point 4

You did not invite your friend to the next concert. Try: Nāc ar mani uz nākamo koncertu!
